Many ordinary people face legal problems but do not know their rights, applicable laws, or what action they can take. Legal information is often complex and difficult to understand, while relevant previous court judgments are scattered and require legal knowledge to research. Because of this, people may accept unfair treatment, make wrong decisions, or delay taking legal action.
Even when people realize that professional help is needed, they often do not know which type of lawyer is suitable for their specific case or how to explain their situation effectively. This lack of legal awareness, accessible case-based information, and suitable lawyer guidance creates a gap between people and the legal support they need.
